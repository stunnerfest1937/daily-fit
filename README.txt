DAILY FIT - put it on your phone and share it
=============================================

This folder is a complete phone app. It has to be put online once, at an
https:// address (free). After that:
  - you open the address on your phone and add it to your home screen
  - it opens like a normal app, even with no internet
  - your friends open the same address and each get their OWN wardrobe, kept
    on their own phone (nothing is uploaded anywhere)

STEP 1: PUT IT ONLINE (pick one)

  A) Netlify Drop (about 2 minutes, easiest on a laptop)
     1. Unzip this folder.
     2. Go to app.netlify.com/drop and sign up for free.
     3. Drag the whole unzipped folder onto the page.
     4. Netlify shows an address. That is your app.

  B) GitHub Pages (works from a phone browser, clunkier)
     1. Unzip this folder using your phone's Files app.
     2. Create a free GitHub account and a new PUBLIC repository.
     3. Add file > Upload files > choose every file in this folder > Commit.
     4. Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main, folder "/ (root)".
     5. After a minute your app is at https://YOUR-NAME.github.io/REPO-NAME/

STEP 2: INSTALL IT
  Android (Chrome): open the address, then tap "Install app" in the setup
                    guide on the Today screen, or menu (three dots) >
                    Install app / Add to Home screen.
  iPhone (Safari):  open the address, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

STEP 3: SHARE IT
  In the app, scroll to the bottom of any screen and tap
  "Share app with friends". It opens your phone's share sheet with the link.
  To post today's look, tap "Share" under your outfit. That sends a picture
  of the outfit to WhatsApp, Instagram or anywhere else.

FIRST RUN
  A short setup guide on the Today screen walks through: your city (for live
  temperature and rain chance), clearing the sample clothes, adding your
  clothes and classes, and installing the app.

PHOTOS
  When you add a piece you can take or choose a photo. The app reads the main
  colour from the picture and picks it for you (you can change it). Photos are
  small and stay on the phone. They are included when you Save backup.

YOUR DATA
  Everything is saved on the phone itself. Use Wardrobe > Save backup now and
  then. "Restore backup" loads that file again, for example on a new phone.
  If you update the app later, upload the new files the same way. Your
  clothes stay on the phone.
