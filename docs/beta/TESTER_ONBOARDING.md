## What is Daily Fit?

Daily Fit is a small app that picks one outfit a day from **your own clothes**. It looks at:
- your timetable (a lab day or a presentation day needs different clothes),
- the weather, if you choose to switch it on,
- colours that go well together,
- what you wore recently, so you don't repeat yourself.

It does not sell clothes or show ads. There is no account to create.

## Step 1: Open it and add it to your home screen (Android)

1. Open **Chrome** on your phone.
2. Go to: **https://stunnerfest1937.github.io/daily-fit/**
3. Wait for the Daily Fit screen to load. You will see a short setup guide on the Today screen.
4. To install: tap **Install app** in the setup guide. If you don't see it, tap the **three dots** at the top right of Chrome and choose **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
5. Open Daily Fit from the new icon on your home screen. It opens like a normal app.

**iPhone:** open the link in Safari, tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.

**If you can't install it**, you can still use it in the browser. Tell the organiser which phone and browser you have. That is useful information for them.

## Step 2: Set it up (about 15 minutes)

The app starts with **23 sample clothes and 11 sample classes** so you can see how it works. Replace them with yours:

1. **(Optional) Weather.** In the setup guide, search for your city, or tap **Use my location**. Or skip this and choose the weather by hand. The app works without it.
2. **Add your own clothes first.** Open the **Wardrobe** tab. In **Add a piece**, type a name (like "Navy polo"), choose the type (top, bottom, shoes, layer or one-piece), the style, the colour, and how warm the fabric is. You can also tap **Take or choose a photo** (optional). The app guesses the colour from the photo, and you can change it. Tap **Add to wardrobe**.
   - There are also **Quick add** buttons for common basics.
   - To get an outfit you need at least **one top, one bottom and one pair of shoes** (or a one-piece and shoes). For a good test add **at least 4 tops, 3 bottoms, 2 pairs of shoes and 1 jacket or hoodie**.
3. **Clear the samples.** Once your own clothes are in, tap **Clear samples**. You'll find it on the setup guide, and also in the Wardrobe and Timetable tabs. Your own pieces stay. If you tap it by mistake, use **Undo** straight away.
4. **Add your classes.** Open the **Timetable** tab, then add the subject, kind of class (lecture, lab, presentation, sports, exam) and the start and end time for each class. Do at least 3 days of the week.
5. **Save a backup.** In the Wardrobe tab, tap **Save backup**. A file downloads to your phone. Keep it. This is your safety copy.

Please note down roughly how many minutes this took. We will ask.

## Step 3: Use it every morning

Open Daily Fit and look at the **Today** screen. You'll see:

- today's outfit, with a few short reasons (for example "Light and breathable for the heat"),
- a **colour match** rating (Safe, Good or Strong).

What you can do:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Shuffle** | Gives a different outfit |
| **Lock icon** next to a piece | Keeps that piece when you shuffle |
| **Swap icon** next to a piece | Changes only that piece |
| **Like** / **Not for me** | Teaches the app what you like |
| **Wear this today** | Saves it as what you wore, so it won't be suggested again soon |
| **Wearing today · Undo** | Takes it back if you tap it by mistake |
| **Save look** | Saves an outfit you love. Find it later in the **Plan** tab |

The **Plan** tab shows the next six days, based on each day's timetable.

You don't have to wear what it suggests! Just tell us honestly whether you would.

## Laundry and repeats

- **Laundry:** in the Wardrobe tab, tap **Wash** next to a piece that is dirty. The app will not suggest it. When it's clean, tap **Clean**.
- **Repeats:** after you tap **Wear this today**, the app strongly prefers other pieces for the next few days. It also won't suggest the exact same full outfit again within 7 days, as long as you have enough clothes to make a different one.
- **Jackets and hoodies:** by default a layer is suggested at most once a week, so your jacket doesn't get worn every day.
- **Small wardrobe:** if you have too few clothes to avoid a repeat, the app tells you ("this repeats a recent look"). Adding a few more pieces fixes it.

## Good to know

- **Your clothes stay on your phone only.** The organiser cannot see them and cannot recover them. If you clear your browser data or uninstall the app, they are gone. **Please save a backup now and again in the middle of the week** (Wardrobe, **Save backup**).
- To restore a backup, tap **Restore backup** and choose the file. The app checks the file first. If something is wrong with the file, nothing is changed. After a restore you get an **Undo** button.
- **Offline:** after you have opened it once online, it still opens without internet. Live weather needs internet.
- If you see a warning at the top that says **"Couldn’t save to this phone"**, tap **Save backup** and tell the organiser.
- There is no sync. A wardrobe on your phone won't appear on your friend's phone or your laptop.

## Your privacy in short

- Your wardrobe, photos, timetable and history are saved only on your phone. The app does not upload them.
- If you use weather, your city name (when you search) and an approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) go to **Open-Meteo**, a free weather service. Nothing about your clothes goes with it.
- The app loads its fonts from **Google Fonts** whenever the page opens, so Google can see that your phone opened a page that uses them.
- Backup files are **not encrypted**. They contain your clothes, timetable and photos, so don't post them publicly.
- The feedback form does not ask for your name, email or phone number.

The full details are in [PRIVACY.md](../../PRIVACY.md).

## If something goes wrong

1. Take a **screenshot** if you can (hide anything personal).
2. Fill in the bug report: [BUG_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md](BUG_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md). Short answers are fine. Please don't send your backup file.
3. Tell the organiser what phone and browser you have.

If you lose your clothes or the app won't open, tell the organiser straight away.

## What you need to do over the 7 days

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Install, set up, save a backup |
| Every day | Open the app, look at the outfit, send the 1-minute daily note (the organiser will show you the format) |
| Day 2 | Mark one piece as **Wash**, check it doesn't appear, then mark it **Clean** |
| Day 3 | Turn on airplane mode and open the app |
| Day 4 | Look at the **Plan** tab |
| Day 5 | Add 2 new pieces (try a photo) |
| Day 6 | Try **Save look** |
| Day 7 | Save a second backup and fill in the feedback form |

You can miss a day. Just write "didn't open".

## Giving feedback at the end

On day 7 the organiser will send you a link to a short form (about 5 minutes). Please answer honestly. The things that annoyed you are the most useful answers.

Thank you for your time. 🙏
